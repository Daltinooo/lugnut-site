Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026.

Lugnut has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or location. Your progress is stored only on your device. The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, and those ads are non-personalized.

What the app stores

  • Your game progress is saved in the app’s local storage on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere and does not sync between devices. Deleting the app deletes that progress, and so does “Hard Reset” in the OFFICE page.
  • There is no login, no account, no analytics SDK and no in-app purchases.

Ads

The only ads in the game are optional rewarded ads: you tap “Watch an ad” to get a short boost. Nothing is ever shown unless you ask for it. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a third-party service run by Google LLC.

  • Non-personalized ads only. The game asks AdMob for non-personalized ads, so ads are not tailored to your interests or your activity in other apps. The game does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use your advertising identifier to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.
  • What AdMob may collect. To deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep the service working, the AdMob SDK may collect and use data such as your device and app identifiers, IP address (which gives a rough location), information about how you interact with an ad, and diagnostic and performance data. We do not receive this data and we do not link it to any account, because the game has none.
  • Consent. Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the game shows Google’s consent form the first time you open it, and no ads are requested until that is settled. You can review or change your choice later under OFFICE → Ad Privacy Choices, which appears in regions where it is required.
  • Learn more about how Google handles ad data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Third parties

The only third-party SDKs in the app are Google’s Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform (the consent form). The links to this policy and to Support open in your browser.

Children

Lugnut is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game itself collects no personal information. Ads shown through AdMob are provided by Google under its own policies.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions? Email coldstartstudios1@gmail.com.
