Privacy Policy

Lugnut collects no data. Progress is stored only on your device.

What the app does

  • It has no accounts, no login and no networking. It never sends anything off your device.
  • It has no analytics, no advertising, no trackers and no in-app purchases.
  • Your progress is saved in the app’s local storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes that progress, and so does “Hard Reset” in the OFFICE page.

Third parties

There are none. The app uses no third-party SDKs.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions? Email coldstartstudios1@gmail.com.
