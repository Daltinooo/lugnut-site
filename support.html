Support

How does saving work?

The game saves automatically every few seconds and whenever you leave the app. Progress lives only on your device, so it does not sync between devices. If the main save is ever damaged, the game falls back to its backup save.

I was away. Where did my cash come from?

The nephew kept working. Offline earnings are capped (2 hours to start). Upgrade the Answering Machine in BLUEPRINT to raise the cap.

How do I reset the game?

Open the address bar, choose OFFICE, then tap Hard Reset and confirm. This erases all progress and cannot be undone.

How do I turn off haptics?

OFFICE has a Haptics toggle under Settings.

Does the game cost anything or show ads?

No. There are no ads and no purchases, and the game works offline.
